GENERAL WELLBEING

Small supportive steps

Pause and observe

Write down when a symptom began, what it feels like, and any changes. Avoid assuming a cause from one symptom.

Rest and hydration

For everyday tiredness or mild discomfort, consider taking a comfortable break and drinking water if you can. Follow any advice already given by your clinician.

Gentle movement

Only if it feels comfortable: try ordinary, gentle movement. Stop if it increases discomfort, causes dizziness, or feels unsafe. Do not push through pain.

Prepare for an appointment

Bring your timeline, medicines/supplements list, relevant health history, and questions. A qualified clinician can assess your individual situation.

Get urgent help
Seek emergency assessment for trouble breathing, chest pain, fainting, new confusion, sudden severe headache, severe allergic reaction, major injury, or any rapidly worsening/severe symptom. This list is not exhaustive.

Information is general education, not personalized medical advice. No medication or treatment is prescribed by this app.
